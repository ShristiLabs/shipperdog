Onboard an organization ​

Every ShipperDog organization is its own walled garden: the tenants, applications, releases, and policies inside it are invisible to every other organization. Creating one is a platform-operator task — the organization is born through the platform API, and everything after that happens in the Console.

Who this is for

Platform operators only. If you received a welcome email, you are joining an organization that already exists — start with your role quickstart instead.

Create the organization ​

  1. Authenticate against the platform API with a token that may create organizations (a platform administrator's).

  2. Create the organization:

    bash
    curl -X POST "$SHIPPERDOG_API/api/v1/organizations" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $PLATFORM_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: onboard-northwind-001" \
  -d '{"name": "Northwind Mobile", "externalId": "northwind"}'

    The response carries the organization's id — quote it for every step that follows. A duplicate externalId is refused (409 EXTERNAL_ID_TAKEN); the idempotency key makes a retried create safe.

  3. You are now the organization's first Organization Administrator — the creator's ORG_ADMIN membership is recorded at creation time. Sign in to the Console to finish the setup before handing it over.

Add the customer's administrator ​

  1. Still as the creator, add the customer's admin as a member of the organization and assign the ORG_ADMIN role (the members API, PUT /api/v1/organizations/{id}/members/{userId}/role).

  2. Share the Console URL and sign-in credentials with them through your usual secure channel. They sign in with email and password until single sign-on is configured for the organization.

  3. Walk them through the first steps every organization needs, in order:

    • Create the first tenants
    • Register the first application
    • Invite testers into a tenant
    • Bind store accounts if they distribute through TestFlight or Google Play

What shapes an organization ​

Once it exists, the organization's settings live across the Console rather than on one screen:

SettingWhere
Members and organization rolesThe members API (role changes audit; the last ORG_ADMIN guard refuses stripping the final administrator)
Single sign-on (OIDC, SAML)SSO setup
User provisioning (SCIM)SCIM provisioning
Access narrowing (ABAC)ABAC policies
Compliance feedsSIEM streams and the audit trail

Deactivating an organization later is reversible and non-destructive: memberships stay, but roles in a deactivated organization grant nothing until it is reactivated. Why organizations are hard-walled this way is covered in tenancy and isolation.
