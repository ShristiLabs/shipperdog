# Browse your applications

Sign in and you are on **My Applications**: one card per app your
team has shared with you. Tap a card to see its builds; that is the
whole screen.

![My Applications - one card per shared app](/screenshots/portal/catalog.png)

## What a card tells you

- **App name** and its **latest version** you are allowed to install.
- A **badge** next to the version saying where that build stands:
  **Draft** (still being prepared — safe to install, earliest
  feedback wins), **Active** (ready and being tested), **Archived**
  (an old build, kept for reference), or **Revoked** (pulled back —
  expect a newer one soon).
- **Platform chips** — `Android`, `iOS` — showing which platforms
  have a build ready. No chip, no build for that platform yet.

**Refresh** re-checks for new builds; if there are more apps than
fit, **Load more** appears at the bottom.

Tap a card and you see the app's builds, newest first — each with its
badge, build number, file size, and upload date, plus the
**Download** and **Report issue** buttons. Every build has its own
shareable page, which is handy when you and the team are talking
about exactly one build.

![A build's page - badge, facts, download, report](/screenshots/portal/version-detail.png)

## If the list is empty

An empty list is not an error. It means nothing has been shared with
you yet — ask your team to add you, then tap **Refresh**. You only
ever see apps you are authorized to test; nothing else exists on
this screen for you.

**Next:** pick a card, then [install on Android](/how-to/portal/install-android.md)
or [install on iOS](/how-to/portal/install-ios.md).
