# Submit feedback

Found something? On any version page (or any build card in the
version history), tap **Report issue**. The form opens with the app,
version, and your identity already attached — you only describe what
you saw.

![The report form](/screenshots/portal/feedback-dialog.png)

## Filling the form

1. **Type** — what kind of report: **Bug**, **Feedback**, **Crash**,
   **Feature request**, **UX issue**, or **Security issue**.

2. **What happened?** — the one required field. What you did, what
   you expected, what went wrong.

3. **Build** — which build you were on. The portal pins the build
   you were looking at automatically; leave **Not build-specific**
   if it applies to all of them.

4. **Device and OS** — optional; the OS is usually filled in for
   you. Correct it if your phone knows better.

5. **Attachments** — screenshots, screen recordings, or logs help
   most. **Add file** accepts `png`, `jpg`, `mp4`, `txt`, and `log`
   files, up to five per report.

Tap **Submit report**. You get a confirmation with the report's
reference — your proof it landed.

::: tip Security reports are private on purpose
If you pick **Security issue**, only the team members cleared to
handle security can see your report — not the whole team. Submit it
the same way; the privacy is automatic.
:::

## What happens after

Your report lands in the team's testing queue with everything you
saw plus the context you never had to type — app, version, build,
device. The team triages it; severe reports can pause a rollout
outright, so early reports matter. If the app you are testing
carries ShipperDog's built-in reporter, a crash can also open this
form pre-filled — you just check it and submit.
