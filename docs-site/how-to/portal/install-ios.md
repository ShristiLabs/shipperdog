# Install on iOS

From any version page, tap **Download iOS build**. The portal checks
you may have this build, then shows one short screen whose wording
depends on how your team distributes the app — the portal tells you
which route you are on, so you never have to guess.

## Direct download

The screen is titled **About direct iOS installs**: tap
**Continue to download** to get the app file (an IPA), then install
it. Direct installs work only for builds your team prepared for
registered devices — if your iPhone refuses the install, the build
was not prepared for your device. Ask your team; they can send it
through TestFlight instead.

## Through TestFlight

The screen is titled **This build is distributed through TestFlight**:
tap **Open TestFlight** and your phone switches to Apple's TestFlight
app, where the build installs after you accept the invitation there.
You will need the TestFlight app on your phone — it is Apple's free
companion app for beta testing, from the App Store.

::: tip
Whichever route, the download link is short-lived — a few minutes.
If it lapses, tap **Download iOS build** again for a fresh one.
:::
