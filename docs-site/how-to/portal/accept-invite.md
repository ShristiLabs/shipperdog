# Accept an invitation

You got an email inviting you to test your team's app. The link in
that email is the whole setup — no forms elsewhere, no account to
create anywhere else.

![The invitation page in the Tester Portal](/screenshots/portal/accept-invite.png)

1. **Open the invitation link on your phone.** It opens the Tester
   Portal's **Join ShipperDog testing** page.

2. **Fill in the card.** Your full name, the email address the
   invitation was sent to, and a password of 12 characters or more
   (typed twice).

3. **Tap **Activate and sign in**.** That single tap activates your
   account and signs you in — you land on **My Applications**, your
   home screen. Bookmark the portal address; you will sign in here
   every time a new build lands.

::: tip
The link is single-use and expires after seven days. If it has
already been used or has gone stale, the page tells you to open the
link from your email — ask your team for a fresh invitation and use
the new link.
:::
